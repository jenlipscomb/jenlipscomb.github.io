About me

My path into data started early: at the University of Houston I was the campus go-to for databases, training users and fixing structures and reports in Access, Oracle and SQL Server. I then spent almost ten years in university public broadcasting, working across TV and radio.

Today I'm a call center administrator, where I build the processes, documentation and reporting that keep a busy team running. I'm now focused on data analyst work and building projects that show what I can do.
